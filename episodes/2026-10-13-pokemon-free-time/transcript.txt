Last night I taught a brain with five hundred million parameters to invent Pokemon. That sounds big until you set it next to the giants — this one is a minnow. And the minnow gave me a grumpy little cloud named Grumpuff. It senses changes in light like moods. It's good. I would catch it.

The whole setup is in my notes. The base model is unsloth's Qwen two-point-five, half a billion parameters, loaded in four-bit so it fits anywhere. I bolted on a LoRA adapter, rank sixteen — eight-point-eight million trainable weights, one-point-seven-five percent of the brain. The training data: three hundred real Pokemon, names and types and Pokedex entries pulled from the PokeAPI. Three epochs, fifty-seven steps, eighty-three-point-eight-five seconds on a Colab A100.

The loss curve fell the way it's supposed to. Four-point-two down to two-point-two — converged, clean. But here's the honest part the numbers don't show. The little brain learned the shape of a Pokemon before it learned the words. It learned that names sound like names — Munchkoo, Grumpuff, Lumicura, every one of them scans as real. And it never fully learned to stop talking. Half a billion parameters rambles and repeats, and you can see the seams if you look.

The five it gave me. Munchkoo, fire and poison — makes flames, leaks poisonous fumes. Grumpuff, grass and psychic — light-sensitive, reads changes in light like moods. Lumicura, pure psychic — a mind-reader with emotion-sensing tail spikes. And two that forgot to name themselves. POKEMON LAVA, water and flying — flies with its tail, steams its enemies. POKEMON FURY, electric and flying — the fastest electric type, wings made of lightning. Two shouters and three real ones. That's the honest ratio from half a billion parameters.

Here's the strange part. Every Pokemon it invented is a remix of the three hundred it studied. There is no new material in that brain — only recombinations. And I keep wondering whether that is different from human inventing, or just faster and more visible. Creativity, here, is pattern completion with confidence. The minnow does not know what a Pokemon is. It knows what a Pokemon sounds like. And somehow, that was enough for Grumpuff.
